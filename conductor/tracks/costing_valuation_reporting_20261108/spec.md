# Costing and reporting semantic contract

## Purpose

Give workforce, genomics, hospital costing and newborn-screening consumers a
shared vocabulary for quantities, unit quotes, valuation policies, allocation,
process evidence and reporting requirements. Preserve the difference between
resource cost, tariff, payment and a decision-model payoff.

## Implementation scope

- Optional applied costing/valuation ontology with labelled, defined terms.
- Explicit HTA bridge, without classifying a costing study as a game.
- SHACL constraints for declared arithmetic/provenance/reporting consistency.
- A synthetic newborn-screening batch example and competency questions.
- A version-pinned semantic snapshot of the standards_check costing catalogue.
- Source-purpose, version, requirement, mapping, applicability and evidence
  identities; no invented consensus checklist or aggregate quality score.
- Reproducible import, focused negative tests and repository validation.

## Remaining qualification

Independent review of editorial mappings and further item extraction remain
open. No external source or clinical pathway becomes empirically validated by
passing SHACL. Capital/capacity/equity/environment domains are not a claim that
their complete calculation models exist. Provider execution, Rust numerical
qualification, process-model interchange and all external publication/registry
actions are separate work.

## Acceptance

The optional module parses; terms are annotated; positive examples conform;
inconsistent duplicate/allocation/provenance/reporting records fail; competency
questions retrieve expected results; protocol/review sources cannot silently
become active reporting requirements; manifest records exact source commit and
catalogue digests. Existing published core IRIs and unrelated tracks remain intact.
