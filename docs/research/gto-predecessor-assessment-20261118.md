## Original 2013 source

The **7th European Conference on Games Based Learning** (Porto, 3–4 October 2013) published *A Domain Ontology of Game Theory Applied to Game Based Learning*, beginning at proceedings **page 666**. The original conference abstract names **Yemna Mejbri, Maha Khemaja and Rafik Braham** and describes a game-theory ontology intended to support strategic-scenario analysis in game-based learning. It lists **WSML** as a keyword. This establishes relevant prior work but not its complete vocabulary or released serialisation.

## 2018 primary follow-up

The [publisher text](https://eudl.eu/pdf/10.4108/eai.4-1-2018.153533) is **CC BY 3.0 for that article only**, not for the 2013 paper or an independently released ontology. Its requirements-ontology figure captions should not be mistaken for a verified original GTO class hierarchy.
