This is coverage of the recorded discovery set, not evidence that every existing game-theory ontology or related approach has been found. The machine-readable companion preserves every seed ID, inherited link, actual verification level and outstanding acquisition task.

The selected twenty-source audit and five research baselines receive focused primary-source checking. Other inherited seeds retain honest title-and-primary-URL citations at level 0; they have no invented creator, publication date, DOI or accessed date. Level 3 indicates corroborated indexed/provider metadata with acquisition limits; level 4 indicates authoritative resolution; level 5 indicates inspection of relevant bibliographic source content. None is a blanket certificate that every claim, theorem, licence or software release has been verified.

hashes of the checked artifacts. It does not upgrade inherited evidence levels.

## How the next paper should compare the five research baselines

| Research baseline | Fair comparison | Necessary boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Compositional game theory | Encode the same small open-game components and compositions; compare resulting strategy/equilibrium conditions and preserved interface information. | Respect the paper’s domain and off-equilibrium best-response assumptions; ontology modularity alone is not a compositional-game theorem. |
| MAID equilibrium refinements | Use matched MAID/EFG fixtures with the same chance, decision, utility and information dependencies; compare supported refinements and query results. | Report restricted game classes, perfect-recall/regularity assumptions and unsupported constructs. |
| Reasoning about Causality in Games | Compare the same explicitly modelled intervention and counterfactual query across causal game representations. | Ordinary execution traces and matching payoffs cannot establish causal equivalence. |
| GameInterpreter | Run matched natural-language prompts/reference games; score actors, action legality, histories, information sets, chance probabilities, utilities and abstention separately. | Pin dataset, prompts, model and human reference; syntax validity is insufficient semantic fidelity. |
| 2026 EFG consistency workshop paper | Apply its instance-relative consistency checker to matched games; compare violations detected, false acceptances and retained information. | Acquire full paper/code first; automatic consistency checking is not proof of a correct general natural-language interpretation. |

These are explicit comparators for preservation/loss, composition, strategic information and formalisation hypotheses. The next paper should report losses and unsupported cases, distinguish static/dynamic decision structure from normal/extensive representation and execution scheduling, and provide evidence before any novelty claim. A GTA-based title becomes a strategic-game example only after actors, available actions, observations/information, timing, objectives and scenario boundaries are specified. Paignia is the designated runtime target; UOGTO remains a language-neutral semantic specification.
